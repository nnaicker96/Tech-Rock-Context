# Tech Rock Context — Start Here

This repository is the dedicated operating context for **Tech Rock**, Rock City Church's digital infrastructure, IT and systems function.

It is intentionally self-contained enough for ChatGPT, Claude, a developer or another collaborator to understand Tech Rock without guessing its relationship to Rock City Church.

## Authority and relationship to Rock City

Tech Rock operates within **ROCK | CITY | CHURCH**. The Rock City context repository remains the broader canonical source for church-wide institutional, ministry, theology and brand context. This repository is the focused canonical context for Tech Rock-specific systems, architecture, governance, technical work and artifact requirements.

Do not treat Tech Rock as an unrelated technology brand.

## Conflict rule

Current explicit instruction > current task source/approved project decision > stable Tech Rock and Rock City constraints > generic best practice.

Never silently replace an established decision with a generic industry convention.

Historical implementation notes are not proof of current production state. Inspect the relevant repository, deployment or connected system before claiming something is live, fixed, deployed or integrated.
