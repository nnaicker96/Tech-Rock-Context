## Kairos 2026–27: Transformation

The current Kairos theme is **Transformation**: God renewing and forming people into Christlikeness, with intentional participation rather than generic self-improvement.

A useful summary is **Revelation → Formation → Function**.

Within the wider movement:
- **Worship** emphasises proximity to God;
- **Discipleship** emphasises intentional covenanting and formation;
- **Fellowship** emphasises Christlike culture and shared life;
- **Ministry** expresses formation through service and community change;
- **Evangelism** draws people into encounter and church life.

This matters to Tech Rock because systems should serve people being formed and ministries functioning well. Technology is infrastructure, not the theological centre.
