# Tech Rock — Operating Model and Governance

## Access principles

Use individual accounts rather than shared credentials. Apply least privilege: people should receive the access needed for their role, not broad access for convenience.

Access lists recorded elsewhere are historical implementation context unless live permissions have been verified.

For security-sensitive changes, confirm the current authorised people and the actual system state before changing access.

## Data and POPIA

Treat personal information, children's information, contact records and exports as controlled data.

Practical operating principles:
- collect only information needed for the workflow;
- avoid unnecessary exports and duplicate datasets;
- restrict administrative access;
- review access when responsibilities change;
- keep credentials out of repositories and documents;
- do not expose personal data merely to make a dashboard convenient.

This context records operational principles and is not legal advice or a complete POPIA compliance programme.

## Kids Rock safeguarding

Check-In workflows are not merely an attendance convenience. Preserve safeguarding and controlled child collection requirements when evaluating QR, app or custom check-in ideas.

Do not replace a secure workflow with a simpler custom flow without confirming that safeguarding requirements remain intact.

## Human approval

Do not automate consequential access, safeguarding, financial or sensitive-data actions merely because an integration technically permits it. Keep appropriate human confirmation in the workflow.

## Support and handover

A Tech Rock system should be understandable by someone other than its original builder. Maintain:
- setup instructions;
- dependencies;
- deployment location;
- configuration variables without secret values;
- troubleshooting notes;
- known limitations;
- recovery or rollback steps where relevant.

Do not report a system as fixed, deployed or connected until that state has actually been verified.
