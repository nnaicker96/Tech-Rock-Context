# Tech Rock — Scope and Responsibilities

## Purpose

Tech Rock is Rock City Church's digital infrastructure, IT and systems function. Its purpose is to make the church's digital tools, workflows and information infrastructure usable, maintainable and aligned with ministry operations.

Tech Rock is not simply a collection of technical projects. It enables the wider church through systems, documentation, governance, testing, support and training.

## Established scope

Tech Rock includes:
- digital infrastructure and church IT;
- Planning Center and Church Center enablement and administration;
- registrations, forms and related digital workflows;
- church websites and purpose-built web tools;
- repositories, technical documentation and versioned context;
- data and integration tooling;
- Media.

Lighting sits under Media.

**Sound is excluded from Tech Rock's organisational scope**, even though Nikyle personally works across sound and media.

## Operating responsibilities

Use this operating pattern when coordinating technical work:

**Coordinate → Delegate → Follow up → Test → Confirm completion**

A request is not complete merely because it was assigned or code was produced. Confirm the intended result in the actual environment where practical.

Tech Rock should:
- enable appropriate systems;
- document configuration and decisions;
- govern access and technical standards;
- test changes;
- maintain supported systems;
- support and train users;
- improve workflows when recurring friction is identified.

## Identity constraints

Do not represent Tech Rock primarily with sound desks, microphones or speakers.

Do not reduce its visual or conceptual identity to generic IT imagery such as circuits, laptops, clouds or gears. Tech Rock exists to build infrastructure for the whole church.

## Boundaries

Planning Center remains the ministry system of record where adopted. Tech Rock enables and governs the implementation; it does not turn every ministry process into a custom application.

The later church-wide administration requirement is broader than the original Tech Rock tracker. Keep the church operations platform direction distinct from the earlier Tech Rock-specific application.
